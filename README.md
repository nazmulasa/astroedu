# 🎓 Astro Edu

### Smart Education Management Software

**Simplify Education. Empower Institutions.**

**Astro Edu** is an education management solution designed to help schools, colleges, madrasas, and other educational institutions streamline academic and administrative operations.

Developed by **[Code Astro](https://www.codeastro.dev)**, Astro Edu aims to make educational administration more organized, efficient, and accessible through technology.

## ✨ Key Features

👨‍🎓 **Student Management** — Organize student profiles and academic records.
📝 **Admission Management** — Manage student admission information and processes.
📅 **Attendance Management** — Track student attendance and maintain records.
📚 **Academic Management** — Organize classes, subjects, and academic sessions.
🧪 **Examination Management** — Manage examination schedules and assessments.
📊 **Results & Reports** — Organize student results and academic performance reports.
👩‍🏫 **Teacher Management** — Maintain teacher profiles and relevant information.
💳 **Fee Management** — Manage tuition fees and other educational payments where supported.
🔐 **Access Control** — Support appropriate permissions for different user roles.

*Features depend on the implemented version and deployment configuration.*

## Who Is Astro Edu For?

- Schools and colleges
- Madrasas and Islamic educational institutions
- Coaching centers
- Private educational organizations
- Academic administrators and teachers
